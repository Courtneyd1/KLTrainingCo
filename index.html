Meet your coach

Training built from experience, not guesswork.

My background in fitness started with powerlifting in 2017, where I went on to win two state bench press titles in New York and Pennsylvania. Since then, I’ve competed as an OCB Figure athlete and currently compete in NPC Bikini.

I started personal training in 2018, and over the years I’ve had the opportunity to work with everyone from everyday clients to high school athletes, including wrestlers, field hockey players, and soccer players.

Competing has taught me a lot about strength, discipline, and what the body is capable of. Coaching is where I get to put that experience to work for someone else. I believe training should be personal. Your goals, your starting point, and your life outside of the gym all matter.
